Hi, I'm Julian. I'm a PhD student at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology, working in the junior research group Numerical methods for nonlinear optics as part of the CRC 1173 Wave phenomena: analysis and numerics.

I stayed on after my Master's in Mathematics, which I finished here in 2023.

Here you'll find a short CV.

Research Interests

  • Analysis and Numerical Analysis of PDEs
  • Time-dependent and time-harmonic wave equations (Maxwell, Helmholtz, wave-heat systems)
  • Discontinuous Galerkin and high-order finite element methods
  • Time integration schemes
  • Rigorous error and stability analysis of space and time discretizations
  • Applications in optics and acoustic / electromagnetic scattering
